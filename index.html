Loupe+ Magnifier

Loupe+ (Lupa+) turns your iPhone into a digital magnifier: automatic zoom as you move the phone away, manual zoom, mirror, light, filters and read-aloud.

We usually reply within a few days.

Frequently asked questions

  • The image does not zoom when I move the phone. Make sure Auto mode is on (blue button at the bottom left). Start with the phone close to the text, then slowly pull it away.
  • I want a fixed magnification. Tap Auto to switch to Manual and use the zoom dial or pinch.
  • The camera does not work. Allow camera access in Settings › Loupe+ › Camera.
  • Read aloud uses the wrong language. In the read sheet, tap “Change” next to the language. Voices can be added in iOS Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.
  • How do I restore Loupe+ PRO? Settings in the app › Loupe+ PRO › Restore purchase (with the same Apple Account).
