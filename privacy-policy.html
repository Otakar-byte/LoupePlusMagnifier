Loupe+ Magnifier

Privacy Policy

Effective: 25 October 2026

In short: Loupe+ collects no personal data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Developer

Petr Paroubek, Czech Republic · pparoubek@gmail.com

Camera

The camera image is processed only on your device to show the magnified view. It is never sent anywhere and is not stored unless you save an image yourself.

Text recognition and read-aloud

Text recognition (Apple Vision) and speech (Apple system voices) run entirely on the device and also work offline.

Saved images

Images you save (Loupe+ PRO) stay only in the app on your device. You can delete them in History or by uninstalling the app. Saving to Photos happens only when you choose to, with your permission.

Purchases

The one-time Loupe+ PRO purchase is processed by Apple. We receive no payment or personal information.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page.
